Student Skill Pack

Nine commands that make Claude Code teach you while it helps.

Why

Cognitive debt is what builds up when AI does the thinking and you only copy the answer: it works today, but you cannot explain it tomorrow. These commands keep you the one who understands.

Your 9 commands

/tutorExplains anything in plain English."I don't understand this error"
/plan-firstResearch, plan, then build."Plan this assignment first"
/fix-itFixes one error and proves it."fix this error"
/designMakes your page look professional."make this page look better"
/sparArgues against your plan."poke holes in my plan"
/prompt-optimizerCleans up a messy idea."turn this brain dump into a prompt"
/token-maxStretches your Pro plan."I am running out of usage"
/handoffSaves your place."save my place"
/skill-builderBuilds your own command."turn this into a skill"

3 hooks: small helpers that run on their own

welcomeStarts each session by showing your commands. About 60 tokens.
terseTells Claude to answer in short plain sentences all session, which saves your plan. About 70 tokens. Say "terse off" to stop it.
guardWatches every terminal command and blocks the dangerous ones: deleting your home folder, force push, hard reset, repo delete. Costs nothing unless it blocks.

Install in 3 steps (plus an optional extra)

  1. Open github.com/centurion-ai-hq/student-skill-pack and copy the text in INSTALL-PROMPT.md.
  2. Paste it into Claude Code.
  3. Pick "Install everything", or choose one by one. Then start a new session.
  4. Optional: Anthropic publishes free official skills. Type /plugin marketplace add anthropics/skills, then /plugin to browse.

Save your $20 plan

  • Type /model sonnet for everyday work.
  • Use plan mode (Shift+Tab) before big tasks.
  • One task per session. Use /handoff, then start fresh.
  • Paste the error lines, not whole files.
